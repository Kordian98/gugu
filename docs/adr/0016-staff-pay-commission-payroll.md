# ADR-0016: Staff pay, commissions, monthly payroll and the owner's profit estimate

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-11-04
- Owner: salon-crm (topic 07)
- Context: owner request 2026-11-04 ("payroll and commissions for staff, plus an estimated monthly profit for the owner"). It supersedes the "no staff commission tracking" line of spec G14 / q21; GuGu itself still takes no commission (X13).

## Decision

1. **Effective-dated pay settings.** `staff_pay_settings` holds versions per person (`effective_from`, base type `none | monthly | daily | hourly`, amount, default commission in basis points) with per-service / per-variant overrides (`staff_commission_overrides`, % or fixed VND per service). The version in force on a date is the latest `effective_from` ≤ that date. Saving a version for an existing date replaces it; any other date adds a version. Past months never change. The commission base is a salon policy with the same history (`salon_commission_policies`, default `price_after_discount`).
2. **Commission per completed booking line**, by `booking_services.staff_id` (never the booking's first staff member). No-shows and cancellations earn nothing. Pending, confirmed and checked-in lines are *projected*. A pending or confirmed line whose start has passed is *stale* and counts as neither. Pure engine in `packages/domain/src/payroll` (unit- and property-tested).
3. **Price after discount.** A booking has no discount field. When the salon records a lower amount at the salon (`offline_sales`), that amount is split over the booking's lines pro rata to their booked prices (largest remainder, so the parts add up exactly) and used as the base. A recorded amount ≥ the booked total (tips, extras) never raises commission. `price_minus_cost` subtracts today's execution cost of the line (service/variant + add-ons, same rule as `margin.estimated`), floored at 0.
4. **Offline sales are not a separate commissionable sale.** `OfflineSale` has no staff of its own: it is the amount for a whole booking whose lines already carry staff. It only adjusts the base (point 3), and is never added on top (no double counting).
5. **Base pay** pro-rated per version: monthly × employed days ÷ days in month (employment starts at the first version and ends on the staff profile's removal day); daily × employed days with scheduled work; hourly × scheduled hours. Schedules come from `staff_schedules` minus breaks and time off (`workIntervals`), so per-day and hourly pay follow the work schedule, not clock-in data (there is none).
6. **Monthly payroll** = base + commission + bonus − deduction − advance. Adjustments need a reason, are soft-deleted, and are audited without the reason text. **Closing** a month stores a snapshot (`payroll_months.snapshot`). After that the month is read-only: adjustments and pay versions inside it return 409 `payroll.month_closed`. Reopening needs a reason and is audited. "Paid on" marks (`payroll_payouts`) exist only on closed months and survive a reopen. All payroll writes take a per-salon advisory lock. This is bookkeeping only: no money moves, and there is no tax or social-insurance calculation. The UI and the export always say the figures are estimates before tax and insurance.
7. **Profit view** (`GET insights/profit`, metric catalogue `profit.*`): earned so far (recorded amount where entered, else booked price) + still to come (line snapshots) − optional no-show allowance − service costs − payroll (full-month base + commission earned + projected + bonuses − deductions; advances are pay, not an extra cost) − other monthly costs (`salon_costs`, recurring or one-off; edits apply from the viewed month, earlier months keep their figures). The no-show allowance uses the salon's own 90-day rate, needs at least 20 finished appointments, and applies to projected revenue, projected service costs and projected commission alike. It is labelled as an assumption (`basis: "assumption"`). A closed payroll month feeds the profit view from its snapshot, so the two screens always agree.
8. **Permissions.** `payroll.read` / `payroll.manage` belong to the owner by default. They are **not** `OWNER_ONLY_PERMISSIONS`, because those can never be granted, and the owner must be able to grant payroll to a manager. A new list `MANAGER_GRANTABLE_PERMISSIONS` lets an override grant them to managers only. Grants to receptionists or staff are ignored. The grant is set through `PUT members/:memberId/payroll-access` (`members.roles.write`, owner-only, audited). `payroll.self.read` is in every role: a member sees only their own estimate, resolved from their own staff profile and never from client input. Anyone who can hold `payroll.read` already holds `services.cost.read` (tested), because the profit view shows service costs.
9. **AI assistant.** One read tool, `month_profit`, returns salon totals and margins only (permission `payroll.read`). Per-person pay, salaries and commissions are personal data and are never exposed (X10). The output schema has no staff dimension, and the eval suite carries a salary canary.

## Consequences

- Dashboards and the assistant read the same `ProfitService`. Tile values are tested against the registry keys, and the assistant output is tested against the Insights tile (parity).
- A team payroll *total* for a one-person salon equals that person's pay. The tool is owner-only, and the owner already knows it. Revisit if the assistant ever opens to more roles.
- Per-day and hourly pay depend on schedules being kept up to date. A salon that sets schedules mid-month gets no working days before that date. The UI shows "N working days" so the owner can check it.
- Service costs use today's cost, not a snapshot at booking time (same as `margin.estimated`). Accepted for an estimate; a cost snapshot on `booking_services` would be needed for exact historic margins.
- Vietnamese social insurance and PIT are out of scope. Whether they should come later is tracker question q98; the no-show default is q99 and the pay total in the assistant for tiny teams is q100.
