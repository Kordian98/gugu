# ADR-0017: Payroll UX redesign, bulk commission changes and the per-person real cost

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-11-03
- Owner: salon-crm (topic 07)
- Context: the owner reviewed the payroll feature of ADR-0016 ("more visible, simpler to edit, more readable") and then asked for a per-person "real cost" ("Chi phí thực tế"): what an employee really costs the salon including taxes, social insurance and other contributions. This also answers q98 (no automatic BHXH/PIT; salons enter the figure themselves).

## Decision

1. **Real cost is part of the pay version.** `staff_pay_settings` gains `employer_cost_bps` (a % of gross) or `employer_cost_amount_minor` (a fixed VND amount per month), both null = not set (CHECK `staff_pay_settings_employer_cost_valid`: at most one, % 0–100, amount ≥ 0). It is effective-dated with the rest of the version, so a change never rewrites earlier months, a closed month keeps its snapshot, and it is audited through `payroll.pay_settings_saved` (the mode only, never the amount). Columns rather than a new table: the value has exactly the same lifecycle as the version it belongs to.
2. **Math** (`employerCostTerms` / `employerExtra`, `packages/domain/src/payroll/payroll.ts`): per employed day the version in force decides; a fixed amount is pro-rated like monthly base pay (amount × days ÷ days in month); a % is weighted by the share of employed days and applied to gross pay (base + commission + bonus − deduction; an advance is part of gross). The extra is never negative. Because the terms are linear, the same terms price projected commission (`projectedEmployerCost`), which the profit view scales by the no-show assumption like the commission itself.
3. **Where it shows.** Payroll rows and totals carry `gross`, `employerCost`, `employerCostSet` and `realCost` (= gross when not set); "to pay" (`total`) stays the gross estimate. The profit view's payroll line uses the real cost (`breakdown.employerCosts`, registry metric `profit.employer_costs`, included in `profit.payroll`). The assistant's `month_profit` payroll total therefore uses real cost — still one salon total. `GET payroll/me` (staff) never carries it (tested). Old snapshots without the fields read as 0 / not set.
4. **GuGu computes no tax or insurance.** The UI says the figure comes from the salon's accountant and that GuGu does not claim it is legally correct.
5. **Bulk commission endpoint** `POST payroll/commission/bulk` (`payroll.manage`): the same default % or own service/variant rate (or its removal) for several people from a date. For each person the version in force on that date is copied with the change (or "commission only" is created when they have none), and the change is also applied to versions already scheduled after that date, so a later version never silently brings the old rate back. One transaction under the payroll lock, one audit entry (`payroll.commission_bulk_saved`, counts only). The rates tab, the service editor and inline edits all use it; the per-person sheet keeps `PUT payroll/staff/:id/pay`.
6. **Small read additions.** `payroll/settings` returns each person's `next` scheduled version (shown as "from 15/11: …"), and `GET payroll/me/lines` gives a staff member their own commission lines (`payroll.self.read`, resolved from their own staff profile; 404 without one) for the "my earnings" list.
7. **UX** (owner review): Payroll has two tabs (month table / rates); the month is one table (person · base · commission earned with "+ expected" · bonus/deduction · gross · real cost · to pay, totals in the footer), cards on a phone; one status chip (open / closed / paid / not started) plus one line explaining the state next to its single action; a row opens a breakdown with every commissioned service (date, rate, amount, link to the appointment). Team cards show the pay setup and open the single pay sheet. "This month" has one headline (estimated net profit) and a waterfall where each bar is split into done (solid) and expected (dashed). The effective date defaults to today with a one-line preview. Removing an own rate, an adjustment or a paid mark has a 6 s undo; closing a month keeps its confirm.

## Consequences

- A % employer cost on a negative gross (deductions larger than pay) adds nothing; acceptable for an estimate.
- Bulk changes propagate into later scheduled versions. Someone who scheduled a deliberate different rate for a later date and then bulk-sets a rate from an earlier date gets the bulk rate there too; the preview line says the change applies "from {date}" and the rates tab shows scheduled versions.
- The export (CSV/XLSX) is unchanged and does not include the real cost yet.
